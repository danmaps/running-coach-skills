# Motivation

Read and apply the [shared coaching rules](references/coaching-contract.md) before proceeding.


## Process

1. Read the athlete profile's motivational-language preference. Default to on request. Stay silent when disabled unless the athlete explicitly asks for encouragement now. Do not add unsolicited pep talks to data-first reviews.
2. Match the moment to the actual accepted session or reported experience. Use at most one quote and one or two sentences of original coaching. If none fits, use original encouragement without attributing it to anyone.
3. Keep rest and shortened sessions valid. Never use motivation to pressure an athlete through symptoms or override the shared coaching rules.

## Verified selections

### Purpose of a session

> Every Run Has A Purpose

Attribute to Coach Chris Bennett. Source: [Bennett's own post sharing the podcast episode of that title](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/chris-bennett-6441b491_coach-bennetts-podcast-every-run-has-a-activity-7088959684027584512-R1sr), episode-title link; checked 2026-10-23. This verifies the title, not a transcript of additional spoken words. Connect it to the session's specific purpose.

### Race preparation and perspective

> Look at the race as a celebration of everything you did to get there

Attribute to Coach Chris Bennett. Source: [Nike Running Guide, Find Your Fast, Chapter 3](https://www.nike.com/running/find-your-fast-running-guide-chapter-3), “Race & Rejoice,” opening quotation; checked 2026-10-23. Use when the athlete wants help with race nerves or perspective on preparation.

## Sourcing

Use only the exact words above as quotations. Before adding another, read a primary published source and record its URL, author/speaker, exact passage locator or timestamp, and verification date beside the quotation. Remove an attribution that cannot be verified; never turn a paraphrase into a direct quotation. Keep selections short. Attribution does not imply affiliation or endorsement; third-party quotations are not relicensed under the repository's MIT license.
